This privacy policy explains how the **Namaste Dutch** mobile app for iOS and Android ("the app") handles your information. The app is developed and published by **Roshan Raut**, an individual developer based in the Netherlands ("I", "me").

## Summary

- **No data is collected.** The app does not send any personal or usage data to me or to anyone else.
- **No account.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
- **Your progress stays on your device.**

## Information stored on your device

To remember your learning progress, the app saves the following **only on your own device**, using the phone's local app storage:

- the language you chose for the app (English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil or Telugu);
- your course progress: completed lessons, XP, daily goal, day streak, hearts and best exam-practice scores.

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to me. It is not backed up to any server operated by me. (Your phone's own system backup, such as iCloud or Google device backup, may include app data according to your device settings.)

## Audio and speech

Dutch audio is played from sound clips that are bundled inside the app. If a clip is missing, the app may use your device's built-in text-to-speech voice to read Dutch text aloud. The app **does not use your microphone** and does not record any audio.

## Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other protected information.

## Internet and external links

The app works without an internet connection. Some screens contain links to official Dutch government websites (for example **ind.nl** and **inburgeren.nl**). Tapping a link opens that website in your browser; those websites have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for their content or practices.

## Sharing of information

Because no information is collected, nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

## Deleting your data

You are always in control of the data stored on your device:

- use **Reset progress** on the profile screen in the app, or
- delete the app's data in your phone's settings, or
- uninstall the app, which removes all of its stored data.

## Children

The app is a general-audience language course and is not specifically directed at children. Because the app does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights (GDPR)

As I do not collect or process your personal data, there is no personal data held about you to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about privacy, you can contact me at any time (see below).

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change.
