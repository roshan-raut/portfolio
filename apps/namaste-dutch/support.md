**Email:** [{{ app.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ app.contact_email }}?subject=Namaste%20Dutch%20support)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I change the app language?

Open the **profile** screen, tap your current language, and pick English, हिंदी, मराठी, தமிழ் or తెలుగు. All lessons, tips and explanations switch to that language.

### I can't hear any audio.

Turn up your phone's media volume. On iPhone the audio plays even when the silent switch is on, so check the volume buttons rather than the switch. If a specific word or sentence has no sound, please email me which one.

### Some words sound slightly different from the main audio.

Almost every word uses a recorded clip. If a clip is missing, the app falls back to your phone's built-in Dutch voice, which can sound different. For the best quality on iPhone, you can download an enhanced Dutch voice in **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Dutch**.

### I ran out of hearts.

Hearts refill automatically: one heart comes back every 30 minutes, up to five.

### How do I start over?

Open the **profile** screen and tap **Reset progress**. This clears your XP, streak and lesson progress on this device and cannot be undone.

### Will my progress move to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on your device — there is no account or cloud sync. It may be restored if you move to a new phone using your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google device backup), but this is not guaranteed.

### Is this the official inburgering exam?

No. Namaste Dutch is an independent learning app and is not affiliated with DUO, the IND or the Dutch government. Exam practice is written in the style of the A2 exam to help you prepare. Always check the official information on [inburgeren.nl](https://www.inburgeren.nl) and [ind.nl](https://ind.nl).

### Does the app collect my data?

No. See the [privacy policy]({{ '/apps/namaste-dutch/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
