Namaste Dutch is a Dutch course for people who think in an Indian language. Every screen, lesson, tip and exam explanation switches to your own language and script.

## What's inside

- **Two levels, 72 lessons** — A1 (12 units) and A2 (12 units). A2 is the level required for the *inburgering* exam, and its units follow everyday life in the Netherlands: work, the doctor, shopping, the gemeente, housing, travel, the bank, school, phone calls and more.
- **Five languages** — English, हिंदी, मराठी, தமிழ் and తెలుగు, each in its own script and font.
- **Real Dutch audio** — bundled clips in a natural Netherlands-Dutch voice, plus an approximate pronunciation written in your own script.
- **Exam practice** in the style of the A2 inburgering exam: reading, listening, KNM, writing and speaking.
- **Citizenship info** — the inburgering requirement for naturalisation explained simply, with links to ind.nl and inburgeren.nl.
- **Keeps you going** — XP, a daily goal, day streaks and hearts; mistakes come back at the end of the lesson.
